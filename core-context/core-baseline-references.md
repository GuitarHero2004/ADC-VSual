# ADC 2026 Core Baseline — Reference List

This file resolves every (Source Year) in-text citation in `core-baseline-context.md`. Grades: Strong (reputable primary, official, or peer-reviewed), Moderate (credible secondary), Weak (single blog, anecdote, or vendor marketing) — as recorded in the underlying research cluster files.

## Problem & Evidence

- **Fan et al. 2021** — general web-visualization accessibility study (N=36 screen-reader users vs. N=36 non-screen-reader users, D3/Google Charts/ChartJS): screen-reader users extract information 61.48% less accurately, spend 210.96% more time, and 33% of visualizations are entirely undiscoverable to screen readers. URL: https://faculty.washington.edu/wobbrock/pubs/assets-21.01.pdf. Grade: Strong.
- **NRTC 2024** — National Research and Training Center on Blindness and Low Vision (Mississippi State University), "Access Technology in the Workplace" longitudinal survey wave (n=219–314 employed BLV screen-reader users): 91.6% use spreadsheet software at work, 70.6% rely on a sighted coworker for inaccessible software, 57.5% report their employer uses screen-reader-inaccessible software (41.3% of it off-the-shelf). URL: https://www.blind.msstate.edu/sites/www.blind.msstate.edu/files/2026-08/NRTC%20AT%20Study%20Final%20Report%202026.pdf. Grade: Strong.
- **NRTC 2026** — same NRTC Access Technology in the Workplace Study, final report 2026, US employed BLV occupation-mix breakdown by BLS category; summing desk/office-coded categories (Management, Office & Admin Support, Computer & Mathematical, Business & Financial Operations, Legal, Architecture & Engineering) yields 55.8% (~56%) of employed US BLV workers in desk/office roles. URL: https://www.blind.msstate.edu/sites/www.blind.msstate.edu/files/2026-08/NRTC%20AT%20Study%20Final%20Report%202026.pdf. Grade: Strong.
- **CHI 2026** — "Making Things Blink," CHI 2026 paper on BLV professionals' GenAI verification practices: users could not detect incorrect data points, chart formatting, or rendering errors even when reviewing the model's own reasoning or underlying code. URL: http://makingthingsblink.com/publications/CHI2026-BLV-GenAI-Verification.pdf. Grade: Strong.
- **ASSETS 2023** — study of 16 blind Excel users: 15 of 16 could not meaningfully extract information from line charts, even with OCR; zero of 16 generated their own charts during the study. URL: https://dl.acm.org/doi/fullHtml/10.1145/3597638.3608423. Grade: Strong.
- **WebAIM 2024** — WebAIM Screen Reader Survey #10 (Dec 2023–Jan 2024, n=1,539): 71.6% navigate by headings when seeking information; proficiency self-ratings (58.3% "Advanced"). URL: https://webaim.org/projects/screenreadersurvey10/. Grade: Strong. Note: the specific 75.1%-of-screen-reader-users-cite-PDFs-as-a-significant-problem figure traces to the earlier WebAIM Screen Reader Survey #8 (n=1,199), not Survey #10 — URL: https://webaim.org/projects/screenreadersurvey8/. Grade: Strong.
- **McDonnall et al. 2023** — McDonnall, Steverson, Sergi, Sessler Trinkowsky, "AT Use in the Workplace" (n=314 employed blind/low-vision): blind professionals use an average of 7 assistive-technology tools (range 1–22); 38.7% dissatisfaction with their screen reader for creating presentations. URL: https://www.blind.msstate.edu/sites/www.blind.msstate.edu/files/2023-05/McDonnall%20et%20al.%20%282023%29%20AT%20Use%20in%20the%20Workplace.pdf. Grade: Strong. (The baseline's meeting screen-share opacity claim is also carried under this citation; the underlying screen-share evidence in the cluster traces separately to Leporini/Buzzi/Hersh, ACM TACCESS 2022, https://doi.org/10.1145/3573012, Strong.)
- **Sharif et al. 2022** — many web charts are intentionally coded so that screen readers skip them entirely; current ARIA support for charts is low. URL: https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2205.04917. Grade: Strong.
- **Chart-to-Text 2022** — Chart-to-Text benchmark (ACL 2022, 44,096 charts, Statista + Pew), image-only variant (no data table available): chart-image OCR extraction accuracy 77.31% before summarization runs. URL: https://aclanthology.org/2022.acl-long.277.pdf. Grade: Strong.
- **Bragg et al. 2021** — Bragg, Reinecke, Ladner, "Expanding a Large Inclusive Study of Human Listening Rates," ACM TACCESS 2021 (n=1,409, LabInTheWild): visually-impaired participants' mean listening rate exceeds sighted participants' (301 WPM vs. 297 WPM in the 2021 replication; the frequently-cited 334 vs. 297 WPM figure is from the original CHI 2018 study this paper extends, Bragg/Bennett/Reinecke/Ladner, https://wildlab.cs.washington.edu/Publications_files/Bragg_CHI2018.pdf). URL: https://dl.acm.org/doi/fullHtml/10.1145/3461700. Grade: Strong.

## Product & Interaction

- **Kim et al. 2023** — Kim, Srinivasan, Kim, Kim, "Exploring Chart Question Answering for Blind and Low Vision Users," CHI 2023 (Wizard-of-Oz, 24 BLV participants, 979 queries): the two most common analytic tasks are Find Extremum and Retrieve Value, together ~40% of all queries. URL: https://pages.cs.wisc.edu/~yeaseulkim/assets/papers/2023_qna.pdf. Grade: Strong.
- **VoxLens 2022** — Sharif et al., "VoxLens," CHI 2022 (controlled study, 21 screen-reader users): voice Q&A over charts yields 122% improvement in information-extraction accuracy and 36% reduction in interaction time versus without VoxLens. URL: https://wildlab.cs.washington.edu/Publications_files/Sharif_CHI2022.pdf. Grade: Strong.
- **VizAbility 2024** — Gorniak, Kim, Wei, Kim, "VizAbility," ASSETS/CHI 2024: LLM-based conversational chart interaction with an explicit raw-data-table fallback (named design consideration D5) and LLM-generated starter questions for cold-start onboarding. URL: https://doi.org/10.1145/3654777.3676414. Grade: Strong.
- **GraphWhisper 2026** — Das, Tarun, Mueller, "Making Charts Speak," CHI/ASSETS 2026: LLM-based conversational chart QA directly from chart images (no pre-structured data required), 98.1% accuracy on visualization-literacy benchmarks, 94% QA accuracy across 185 questions with 15 BLV participants. URL: https://doi.org/10.1145/3772363.3799030. Grade: Strong (graded Moderate/peer-reviewed-adjacent in one secondary cluster mention; Strong per the primary cluster-15 entry, noting the paper is very recent with no independent replication yet).
- **Morae 2025** — Peng, Li, Bigham, Pavel, "Morae: Proactively Pausing UI Agents for User Choices," 2025: an agent that pauses at decision points to let the user choose beat baseline full-automation agents, including OpenAI Operator, on both task completion rate and preference match. URL: https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2508.21456. Grade: Moderate-Strong (rigorous field study; preprint, formal peer-review venue not confirmed).
- **WCAG G155** — W3C WAI, "G155: Providing a checkbox in addition to a submit button," an official tested WCAG technique requiring a distinct confirmation checkbox separate from the submit control for irreversible transactions. URL: https://w3c.github.io/wcag/techniques/general/G155. Grade: Strong. (Related: WCAG 2.2 SC 3.3.6 Error Prevention (All), interpreted at https://a11ybot.app/learn/3-3-6-error-prevention-all/, Strong secondary interpretation.)
- **Verifiable Action Card 2026** — "The Verifiable Action Card," arXiv:2609.18411, Sep 2026: human-in-the-loop confirmation UI must be reconstructed from the ground-truth pending action and rendered in trusted chrome, not from the model's own narration, to resist spoofing via indirect prompt injection. URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18411. Grade: Moderate (rigorous preprint, not BLV-specific but structurally load-bearing).
- **OLLA 2026** — Kodandaram et al., "Are We There Yet? Assessing Computer-Use Agents for Blind Users' Accessible Interaction with Desktop Applications," Sep 2026 (3-week diary study, 8 blind users, 1,258 real commands across 12 apps): best model (GPT-5) achieved only 52.5% success rate. URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00524. Grade: Strong (rigorous diary-study design; preprint, formal venue not yet confirmed).
- **crypto-wallet study 2023** — "Iterative Design of An Accessible Crypto Wallet for Blind Users," arXiv:2306.06261, Jun 2023: a too-costly word-by-word confirmation flow for a 12-word recovery phrase led blind users to skip verification entirely via a "remind me later" option, creating downstream recovery failure. URL: https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2306.06261. Grade: Strong.
- **overlay evidence 2026** — accessibility-overlay cautionary evidence: over 70% of screen-reader users report overlays make websites harder to use; FTC fined accessiBe $1M in 2025 for false compliance claims; 600+ accessibility professionals signed a public rejection statement. URL: https://halfaccessible.com/accessibility-overlays-ada-lawsuits/. Grade: Strong. Primary FTC order: https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2025/04/ftc-approves-final-order-requiring-accessibe-pay-1-million. Grade: Strong.
- **W3C ARIA 2757** — open, unresolved W3C ARIA working-group issue: there is no standard mechanism to tell a screen reader "this element is being narrated by something else, don't talk over it." URL: https://github.com/w3c/aria/issues/2757. Grade: Strong (for "this is unsolved"; any proposed resolution in the thread is Weak/not adopted).

## Competitors & Landscape

- **Be My Eyes 2026** — Be My Eyes Workplace launched 11 Feb 2026, priced $55–90/seat/month (Workplace tier $55–60, Enhanced tier $82.50–90, monthly vs. annual billing); vendor states it is "not a screen reader" and complements existing assistive technology; 1M+ blind/low-vision users, 150+ countries, 10M+ volunteers as of March 2026, with fresh funding raised ($6.1M, Jan 2025) to expand the AI-powered products. Pricing URL: https://www.bemyeyes.com/business/workplace-pricing/. Users/funding URL: https://www.bemyeyes.com/news/be-my-eyes-reaches-1-million-blind-and-low-vision-users-and-10-million-volunteers/. Launch URL: https://www.bemyeyes.com/business/news/be-my-eyes-announces-new-workplace-accessibility-tools/. Grade: Strong (primary vendor pages and press releases).
- **Seeing AI 2024** — Microsoft Seeing AI User Manual Rev. C (Microsoft Garage, Mar 2024): "Seeing AI is not designed to be and should not be used as a primary or exclusive technology by which persons who are blind or otherwise visually impaired learn about their environment" — the explicit complement-not-replacement framing. URL: https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/garage/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Seeing-AI-User-Manual-Rev.-C-13-Mar-2024.pdf. Grade: Strong.
- **Aira 2026** — Aira User Guide, "AI Glasses from Meta": invocation is on-demand via a button press (or paired Meta AI glasses), not a wake word. URL: https://airatechcorp.my.site.com/helpcenter/s/article/User-Guide-Meta-Aira. Grade: Strong (official vendor documentation).
- **JAWS 2025** — Vispero/Freedom Scientific 2025–2026 pricing: JAWS Professional/Work edition (Perpetual + 2-yr SMA) $2,316.50, versus a discounted Home edition as low as $104.50/yr for US blind individuals — roughly a 10–20x gap. URL: https://vispero.com/news/freedom-scientific-announces-changes-to-us-software-pricing/. Grade: Strong (corroborated across two independent reseller listings, emeraldcoastvisionaids.com and accessingenuity.com).

## Market, Buyer & Pricing

- **Lancet 2021** — "Global economic productivity losses from vision impairment and blindness," eClinicalMedicine (Lancet), 2021, GBD-based model: 160.7 million working-age people globally are blind (18.1M) or have moderate-to-severe vision impairment (142.6M). URL: https://www.thelancet.com/journals/eclinm/article/PIIS2589-5370(21)00132-2/fulltext. Grade: Strong.
- **AFB 2024** — American Foundation for the Blind, tabulating US Census ACS data, 10-year series 2016–2025: US employment rate for working-age people with vision difficulty is 44% (2024) to 47% (2025), versus 74–75% for the no-disability population. URL: https://afb.org/research-and-initiatives/statistics/employment-bvi. Grade: Strong.
- **Market.us 2024** — AI-in-accessibility market report: $4.2B (2024) growing to $52.36B (2034), CAGR 28.7%. URL: https://market.us/report/ai-in-accessibility-market/. Grade: Moderate.
- **Precedence 2025** — Precedence Research, digital-accessibility software market: $878.45M (2025) growing to $2,330.57M (2035), CAGR 10.25%. URL: https://www.precedenceresearch.com/digital-accessibility-software-market. Grade: Moderate.
- **Mordor 2025** — Mordor Intelligence, digital-accessibility software market: $0.79B (2025) to $0.85B (2026), growing to $1.15B (2031), CAGR 6.23%. URL: https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/digital-accessibility-software-market. Grade: Moderate.
- **JAN 2025** — Job Accommodation Network cost-of-accommodation survey (updated Sept 17, 2025; employers who contacted JAN Jan 2019–Dec 2024, n=26,028 surveyed / 1,425 reporting cost detail): 61% of accommodations cost nothing; 33% incur a one-time cost, median $300 (explicitly including screen-reading software); 6% incur an ongoing cost, median $2,400/yr. URL: https://askjan.org/topics/costs.cfm. Grade: Strong.
- **EEOC 2026** — three 2026 EEOC settlements over screen-reader-inaccessible workplace software, each requiring specific technical remediation: American Airlines $200,000 (Aug 2026); Pearson Education $150,000 (Aug 2026); PepsiCo $270,000 (Apr 2026), with PepsiCo's own internal estimate that reactive retrofitting would cost up to $1 million and take a year. Primary EEOC press releases: American Airlines https://www.eeoc.gov/newsroom/american-airlines-pay-200000-eeoc-disability-discrimination-suit-following-termination ; Pearson Education https://www.eeoc.gov/newsroom/pearson-education-pay-150000-eeoc-disability-discrimination-suit ; PepsiCo https://www.eeoc.gov/newsroom/pepsico-pay-270000-eeoc-disability-discrimination-suit. The PepsiCo up-to-$1M-and-a-year reactive-remediation estimate is reported in HR Dive https://www.hrdive.com/news/pepsico-settles-eeoc-lawsuit-alleging-it-failed-to-accommodate-and-fired-bl/817857/. Grade: Strong (primary EEOC press releases plus court consent decree, cross-checked via HR Dive).
- **EAA 2025** — European Accessibility Act (Directive (EU) 2019/882): adopted 2019, transposed 2022, enforceable EU-wide since 28 June 2025; extraterritorial, reaching any operator serving EU consumers regardless of location. URL: https://conformi.eu/en/knowledge/32019L0882. Grade: Strong.
- **VPAT/ACR evidence 2026** — accessibility conformance reports (VPAT/ACR) are becoming a pre-award procurement qualification rather than a post-purchase remediation item, driven by EAA enforcement (Forbes Business Council contributed piece, 2026-03-26) and practitioner accounts of procurement teams demanding evidence over marketing claims (know-the-ada.com, 2026-07-01). Grade: Moderate. URL: in research cluster (not located in this pass — only publication names given, no full article URLs recorded).
- **ACCES-VR policy** — NY ACCES-VR Rehabilitation Technology Procedure (1370.00P): state vocational-rehabilitation agencies fund assistive technology that is portable across job sites, but generally will not fund accommodations fixed to one employer's workstation, which remains the employer's obligation. Grade: Strong (primary state-agency policy text). URL: in research cluster (not located in this pass).
- **AFB 2026** — American Foundation for the Blind, "Working with the Machine: AI's Expanding Role in Employment for People With Disabilities" (June 2026, survey co-designed with 19 disability organizations): only 43% of surveilled workers were told about workplace AI surveillance before it began; some accommodations (flexible breaks, remote work) were misread by monitoring tools as underperformance. URL: https://afb.org/research-and-initiatives/ai-series/working-machine. Grade: Strong.

## Vietnam & Region

- **Central Eye Hospital 2024** — Vietnam National Institute of Ophthalmology (Bệnh viện Mắt Trung ương) clinical estimate, cited Oct 2024: approximately 2 million Vietnamese are blind or have poor eyesight; over 80% of blindness is preventable or treatable. URL: https://www.vietnam.vn/en/viet-nam-hien-co-khoang-2-trieu-nguoi-mu-va-thi-luc-kem. Grade: Strong (as recorded in the sourcing cluster).
- **GSO 2023** — General Statistics Office of Vietnam, Vietnam Disability Survey 2023 (2nd national disability survey, released Nov 2024): labor-force participation for people with disability is 23.5% versus 76.3% for non-disabled; vision, communication, upper-body-mobility, and neurological/psychiatric disabilities have employment rates ≤10%, the lowest of all categories; only about a third (~33–35%) of people with disability are online. URL: https://www.gso.gov.vn/tin-tuc-thong-ke/2024/11/thong-cao-bao-chi-ve-ket-qua-dieu-tra-nguoi-khuyet-tat-nam-2023/. Grade: Strong.
- **VBA 2024** — Vietnam Blind Association (Hội Người mù Việt Nam), per Vice Chair Đinh Việt Anh: of 72,000+ members, only about 20,000 (~28%) regularly use a computer or smartphone. URL: https://vneconomy.vn/techconnect/tao-sinh-ke-so-de-nguoi-khuyet-tat-tang-thu-nhap-ben-vung.htm. Grade: Strong.
- **MSD and LNOB 2026** — Institute of Management for Sustainable Development (MSD) + "Leave No One Behind" (LNOB) Vietnam, "Digital Competence of People with Disabilities in Viet Nam" / "Digital Voices" project (published 18 April 2026, Citizen-Generated Data method, n=201 across 5 provinces): 91% of surveyed people with disability own a digital device, but usage stays basic; 52% don't know how to use AI tools, named as an emergent "new digital divide." URL: https://msdvietnam.org/digital-voices-report/. Grade: Strong.
- **UNDP and MOLISA 2023** — joint UNDP + MOLISA "Employment for Persons with Disabilities in Vietnam" study: 93.4% of Vietnamese people with disability aged 16+ hold no technical or professional qualification; only 6.5% hold a diploma or certificate. URL: https://tbh.today/low-employment-participation-among-disabled-individuals-in-vietnam/. Grade: Moderate (secondary write-up of the named UN/government joint study). Independently corroborated at the identical 93.4% figure by Nguyen, Alexander & Bellon (2025), "Exploring the Employment Experiences of People with Physical Disabilities in Vietnam," Disabilities 5(1):10 (MDPI, peer-reviewed), https://doi.org/10.3390/disabilities5010010, Grade: Strong.
- **VBA and SBV 2026** — July 2026 survey of 187 blind/low-vision respondents by the Vietnam Blind Association, State Bank of Vietnam payments department, and UNDP: documented banking-app and ATM screen-reader incompatibility, inconsistent physical keypad layouts, and biometric-authentication friction. URL: https://baotuyenquang.com.vn/xa-hoi/202607/go-nut-that-tiep-can-dich-vu-ngan-hang-so-cho-nguoi-khuyet-tat-36f701d/. Grade: Strong.
- **ASEAN 2025** — ASEAN Secretariat, ASCC Research & Development Platform, Trend Report No. 16 (2025), "Removing Barriers and Gaps for Persons with Disabilities' Participation in the World of Work": frames digitalization as a double-edged megatrend across all 10 ASEAN member states, including Vietnam. URL: https://asean.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/ASCC-RD_Trend-Report_FoW-16_2025_Removing-Barriers-and-Gaps-for-Person-with-Disabilities-Participation-in-the-World-of-Work.pdf. Grade: Strong. Malaysian peer-reviewed transfer evidence: Teo & Subramaniam, "Challenges in Integrating Visually Impaired Persons into Employment in Malaysia," IJIE 2025 (a blind telemarketer describes job-portal/screen-reader incompatibility), https://doi.org/10.22452/ijie.vol17no4.1. Grade: Strong.
